Shooter lab

The roblox-combat kit's first-person and over-the-shoulder camera, gun rules and server checks, ported line for line from Luau. node lab/run-tests.cjs replays every scenario through the real .luau modules and checks each number matches.

First / third personV · D-pad up
Swap shoulderQ · R3
Weapon1–4 · LB/RB

Things to try

Back into a wallThe camera pulls in, never through it, and eases back out.
Hug the right wallThe shoulder offset squeezes; press Q for the open side.
Peek the cornerFrom behind the grey corner the camera sees the dummy but the gun hits the wall: a red dot shows where.
Stand in the bushIt's CanCollide off: the camera ignores it and your shots skip it behind you.
Hold fireRecoil climbs, bloom widens the crosshair, letting go recovers both.
Hip-fireThe character turns to the crosshair for 0.6 s, then runs free again.
